# Presentation Scripts — Challenges, Conclusion & Further Work

**Team Codexon** · Short narrative scripts (~20–30 seconds each)  
Use after the three module sections, before Q&A.

---

## CHALLENGES (~25 sec)

Our main constraints are data grain and scope. Cases are district-level weekly reports, so sub-district detail and reporting delays remain imperfect. Climate is from district point samples, not full spatial fields. Module 2 has very few holdout outbreak weeks, and Module 3 works with only twenty-five districts. The dashboard is a research prototype for decision support — not a certified surveillance system.

**Approx. word count:** ~55 words

---

## CONCLUSION (~25 sec)

We built a Residual Compensation Modeling Framework with three complementary modules: Module 1 forecasts weekly cases, Module 2 classifies calibrated outbreak risk, and Module 3 maps spatial concentration. The Streamlit dashboard brings these views together. The contribution is an integrated, leakage-aware architecture for early-warning support — not a single best model or an operational deployment.

**Approx. word count:** ~50 words

---

## FURTHER WORK (~25 sec)

Next steps include better reporting-delay handling in Module 1, label and threshold review for Module 2 under changing prevalence, and finer spatial data and hotspot metrics for Module 3. At framework level, prospective alert review and validation on later epidemic seasons would strengthen the platform as a decision-support research tool.

**Approx. word count:** ~45 words
